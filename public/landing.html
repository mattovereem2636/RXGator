Why RXGator?
No single discount card is cheapest for every drug at every pharmacy. The winning strategy is to check them all — but who has time for that?

Why not just use GoodRx? Because GoodRx only shows GoodRx prices. We search GoodRx, SingleCare, Cost Plus Drugs, Amazon, Walmart, Costco, Rx Outreach, and 6 government databases — all at once. Sometimes GoodRx wins. Sometimes it doesn't. Shouldn't you know?

Managing medications for a family member? RXGator helps caregivers compare prices across all platforms — search, print, share, and show results to the pharmacist in English or Spanish.
