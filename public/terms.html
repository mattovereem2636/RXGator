4. Pricing Accuracy

RXGator aggregates pricing data from multiple sources including live APIs, government databases, and cached data from third-party platforms. While we make reasonable efforts to display accurate and current pricing:

  • Prices are estimates. Actual prices at the pharmacy may differ from what is displayed on RXGator due to timing, location, insurance status, pharmacy policies, and other factors.
  • Prices change frequently. Drug prices can change daily or even within a single day. Cached data (such as SingleCare pricing) may be hours or days old.
  • Always verify the price at the pharmacy counter before filling a prescription. RXGator is a starting point for comparison, not a final price guarantee.
  • NADAC benchmark prices represent what pharmacies pay on average for medications, not what consumers pay. These are reference points, not retail prices.
  • Costco Pharmacy estimates are calculated approximations based on published reporting, not live pricing data.
