A Tale of Two States

James takes gabapentin 300mg three times a day for nerve pain after shingles. When he moved from Ohio to Virginia, his new pharmacist told him something unexpected: gabapentin is a controlled substance here. His old pharmacy in Ohio had filled it like any other generic. Same drug, different rules — and a different price at the counter.

James isn't alone. Gabapentin is one of the most prescribed medications in the United States — roughly 69 million prescriptions per year, placing it consistently in the top 10 most dispensed drugs nationally. It treats epilepsy, postherpetic neuralgia, restless legs syndrome, and a wide range of off-label nerve pain conditions. It is also one of the most confusing drugs to price.

Unlike most generics, gabapentin pricing doesn't follow predictable patterns. The cost varies dramatically by dosage strength, by pharmacy, by state, and by whether you use a discount program. A 90-day supply of 300mg capsules can range from under $11 with a coupon to over $75 at the retail counter — a seven-fold difference for the exact same pills.

This guide breaks down every factor that affects what you'll actually pay.

What Does Gabapentin Cost at the Pharmacy?

The average retail price for generic gabapentin 300mg, 90 capsules, is approximately $75.25 without insurance or discount programs. That's the "sticker price" — what the pharmacy charges if you hand over a prescription and nothing else.

Almost nobody should pay that price. Discount programs, pharmacy shopping, and manufacturer competition have pushed the actual cash price far below retail for most patients. But the gap between the retail price and the best available price is wider for gabapentin than for many comparable generics, which means the savings from comparison shopping are unusually large.

Key Takeaway

Gabapentin 300mg (90 capsules) ranges from about $10.80 with a GoodRx coupon to $75.25 at full retail. The pharmacy you choose matters more than almost any other factor.

The brand-name version, Neurontin, still exists but costs upwards of $900 for the same quantity. There is no clinical reason to pay for the brand — the FDA has rated all major generic manufacturers as therapeutically equivalent (AB-rated) to Neurontin.

The Scheduling Map: Where Gabapentin Is Controlled

Gabapentin is not a federally scheduled controlled substance. The DEA does not classify it under any schedule. However, individual states can and do impose their own scheduling rules, and this is where James ran into trouble.

As of August 2026, eight states classify gabapentin as a Schedule V controlled substance:

Michigan previously scheduled gabapentin but removed the classification in 2024, citing the administrative burden on pharmacies and minimal evidence that scheduling reduced misuse.

Montana is the newest addition, scheduling gabapentin in late 2025 following a legislative session focused on substance monitoring.

What Scheduling Means for You

In scheduled states, gabapentin prescriptions are tracked through the state's Prescription Drug Monitoring Program (PDMP). Refill limits apply — typically no more than five refills within six months. Prescription transfers between pharmacies may be restricted. Some pharmacies in scheduled states charge a slightly higher dispensing fee to account for the additional compliance paperwork, which can add $2 – $5 to your out-of-pocket cost.

If you live in a scheduled state, plan ahead. You cannot call a pharmacy and ask them to transfer the remaining refills from another state the way you can with non-controlled medications. Your physician may need to write a new prescription, and the pharmacy will run a PDMP check before dispensing.

Dosage Pricing Breakdown

Most generic drugs cost roughly the same regardless of strength — a 10mg atorvastatin tablet costs about the same as a 40mg tablet, for instance. Gabapentin is different. There is meaningful price variation across dosage strengths, and this can affect your total cost depending on how your prescription is written.

400mg $60 – $80 $11 – $16 Capsule
600mg $70 – $100 $12 – $20 Tablet
800mg $80 – $120 $14 – $24 Tablet

Notice that the 100mg capsule is the cheapest per unit but may not be the cheapest per milligram. If you take 300mg three times daily (900mg/day), one 300mg capsule three times a day is typically cheaper than three 100mg capsules three times a day (nine capsules versus three). Talk to your pharmacist about the most cost-effective way to fill your specific dose.

The 600mg and 800mg tablets — note these are tablets, not capsules — tend to have the highest retail prices. However, patients taking high doses often find that fewer tablets per day offsets the per-unit cost. A patient taking 2,400mg daily could fill ninety 800mg tablets (one tablet three times daily) for less than one hundred eighty 400mg capsules covering the same total dose.

Discount Programs and Coupons

The gap between retail and discounted gabapentin pricing is among the widest of any generic, which makes using at least one discount program essential.

GoodRx

GoodRx consistently offers the lowest coupon prices for gabapentin across major chains. For 300mg, 90 capsules, GoodRx coupons bring the price as low as $10.80 at select pharmacies. Prices vary by location — the same coupon may show $10.80 at one CVS and $14 at another based on regional pharmacy pricing contracts.

SingleCare

SingleCare is particularly competitive at CVS and Walgreens, often pricing gabapentin 300mg (90 capsules) between $12 and $18. SingleCare coupons are also accepted at some independent pharmacies that don't participate in GoodRx's network.

Walmart $4/$10 List

Walmart's discount generic program includes some gabapentin configurations at the $4 (30-day) or $10 (90-day) price tier. Availability of specific strengths on this list can vary, and it is worth confirming with your local Walmart pharmacy before assuming your exact dose and quantity are covered.

Costco Pharmacy

Costco pharmacies are open to non-members for prescription fills in most states. Gabapentin 300mg (90 capsules) typically runs $10 to $14 at Costco, making it competitive with the best coupon prices at other chains — without needing a coupon at all.

Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drugs

Cost Plus Drugs uses a transparent pricing model: manufacturer cost plus a 15% markup plus a flat $5 pharmacist fee, plus shipping. For gabapentin 300mg, this often results in a total under $8 for a 90-day supply. The tradeoff is mail-order delivery, typically 5 to 10 business days, which may not work for patients needing the medication immediately.

NADAC: What Pharmacies Actually Pay

The National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC) is a benchmark published by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). It reflects the average price that pharmacies pay their wholesalers for a drug — the actual acquisition cost before any markup.

For generic gabapentin 300mg capsules, the NADAC is approximately $0.03 to $0.05 per capsule. That means a pharmacy acquires 90 capsules for roughly $2.70 to $4.50.

When the retail counter price is $75.25 for the same 90 capsules, that represents a markup of approximately 1,500% to 2,700% over acquisition cost. This isn't unusual for low-cost generics — pharmacies apply higher percentage markups to inexpensive drugs partly because the fixed costs of dispensing (pharmacist time, compliance, overhead) don't scale down with drug cost. A pharmacist spends the same time verifying and dispensing a $3 drug as a $300 drug.

Why This Matters

The extreme gap between acquisition cost and retail price is exactly why discount programs can offer gabapentin for $10 – $15 and still leave margin for the pharmacy. There's a lot of room between $4.50 and $75.25. Any price you negotiate within that range is sustainable for the pharmacy.

Gabapentin vs. Pregabalin (Lyrica): The Price Gap

Pregabalin (brand name Lyrica) is in the same drug class as gabapentin — both are gabapentinoids — and treats many of the same conditions. Patients and prescribers sometimes weigh one against the other, especially when insurance formularies favor one over the other. The price difference matters if you're paying out of pocket.

Factor Gabapentin 300mg (90 caps) Pregabalin 75mg (90 caps)
Average Retail ~$75 ~$120 – $180
Best Coupon Price ~$10 – $15 ~$25 – $45
Federal Schedule Not scheduled Schedule V (all states)
Dosing Frequency Typically 3 times daily Typically 2 times daily
Generic Available Yes (since 2004) Yes (since 2019)

Generic pregabalin has been available since 2019, but its price remains substantially higher than gabapentin — typically two to four times more, depending on the pharmacy and discount program. If cost is a primary concern, gabapentin is the more affordable option. However, pregabalin's twice-daily dosing and potentially faster onset may be worth the higher price for some patients. This is a clinical decision to make with your prescriber, not purely a cost decision.

One important regulatory distinction: pregabalin is a Schedule V controlled substance in every state under federal law. Gabapentin carries that classification in only eight states. This means pregabalin prescriptions always have refill limits and PDMP tracking, regardless of where you live.

How to Pay the Least for Gabapentin

Based on the pricing data above, here is a practical approach to minimizing your gabapentin cost:

  1. Ask for a 90-day supply. The per-capsule price drops significantly compared to 30-day fills, and you make fewer pharmacy trips.
  2. Compare at least three pharmacies. Gabapentin pricing varies more between pharmacies than most generics. Costco, Walmart, and whichever chain gives the best GoodRx price should all be on your list.
  3. Consider mail order. Cost Plus Drugs and similar transparent-pricing mail pharmacies offer the lowest absolute prices, often under $8 for 90 capsules.
  4. Discuss dosage form with your pharmacist. If your total daily dose can be achieved with fewer, larger-strength tablets, the total cost may be lower.
  5. Check your state's scheduling status. If you live in a scheduled state, factor in potential refill restrictions and plan your pharmacy visits accordingly.
  6. Never pay the brand price. Neurontin offers no clinical advantage over generic gabapentin. The generic has been available for over 20 years with well-established bioequivalence.
