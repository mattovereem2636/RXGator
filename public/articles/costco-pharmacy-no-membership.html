Costco Pharmacy Without a Membership — What to Know

You don't need a Costco card to use the pharmacy. Costco's prices on generics are among the lowest in the country. Here's how it works.

Here's a fact that most people don't know: you do not need a Costco membership to use the Costco pharmacy. By law in most states, warehouse club pharmacies must serve the general public for prescription medications. You can walk into a Costco, go directly to the pharmacy counter, and fill a prescription — no membership card required.

And Costco's prices on generics are consistently among the lowest in the country.

Why No Membership Is Required

State pharmacy laws in most of the U.S. require that pharmacies be accessible to the public. Since Costco operates licensed pharmacies, they cannot restrict access to members only for prescription services. This applies to prescription medications specifically — you'd still need a membership to buy the jumbo pack of paper towels on your way out.

At the entrance, simply tell the greeter you're going to the pharmacy. They'll let you through. Some locations may ask you to go directly to the pharmacy area without browsing the store, but you will not be turned away.

How Costco's Pricing Compares

Costco negotiates drug prices aggressively, and because they operate on a membership model rather than maximizing per-transaction profit, their pharmacy margins are thin. The result: generic drug prices at Costco are routinely 30% to 50% lower than CVS, Walgreens, or Rite Aid — and sometimes lower than Walmart's $4 list.

Costco also operates a Costco Member Prescription Program for members that offers additional savings on certain medications. But even without a membership, the standard pharmacy pricing is competitive.

The comparison people don't expect: On many common generics, Costco's non-member cash price is lower than what you'd pay with a GoodRx coupon at CVS. This isn't universally true — check your specific medication — but it's worth looking at Costco before assuming a discount card is your best bet.

How to Check Costco Prices

Costco doesn't publish its pharmacy prices online for non-members in the same way GoodRx shows coupon prices. The most reliable way to check is to call your local Costco pharmacy and ask for the cash price on your specific medication and dose. They'll tell you over the phone.

Costco's advantage is that it's a full-service walk-in pharmacy with broad drug coverage and consistently low pricing. The disadvantage is limited locations — if you don't live near a Costco, it's not practical for regular fills. And unlike Walmart's flat $4 pricing, Costco's prices vary by drug, so you need to check each medication individually.

Tips for Using Costco Pharmacy

Costco pharmacies tend to have shorter hours than chain pharmacies — many close by 7 PM on weekdays and have limited weekend hours. Plan accordingly. They do accept most insurance plans, so you can compare your insurance copay against the cash price and choose whichever is lower. And if you're already a Costco member, the Member Prescription Program stacks on top of already-low pricing, making it an even better deal.
